Sole Mates

The wedding shoe game — for the two of you, on any two devices

What is this?

It's the classic wedding shoe game, online. At weddings, the couple sits back to back, each holding one of their own shoes and one of their partner's. The host asks questions like "Who's the better cook?" and both raise the shoe of the person they think it is. Matching answers mean you really know each other — mismatches mean a fun argument.

How to play

  1. One of you creates a room and gets a 4-letter code. Share it (or the invite link) with your partner — they can join from any phone, tablet or computer.
  2. Each round, one of you is the asker: write your own "who" question, or tap 🎲 Surprise me to draw one from the deck. The asking turn swaps every round.
  3. You both answer the same question — even the asker! Tap the shoe of the person you think it is, then Lock it in. You can see that your partner has locked in, but never what they picked.
  4. When both answers are locked: 3… 2… 1… both shoes are revealed at the same time. Same shoe = 💍 a match and a point. Different shoes = 😅 agree to disagree.
  5. Keep going as long as you like — the ❤️ counter tracks your matches.

Who's who

Whoever creates the room plays as the rose heel; whoever joins plays as the slate oxford.

Room creator 👠 Joiner 🎩
House rules we recommend:

Play to 10 rounds. Every mismatch must be argued about (lovingly) before the next round. The player with fewer matched answers makes breakfast. 🥞

Good to know

Rooms are temporary — they live while you play and are cleaned up about an hour after you stop. If your connection drops, just keep the tab open or reload; you'll rejoin the same room automatically. Sounds can be muted with the 🔊 button.
